IMPORTANT INFORMATION FOR SEARCHING

Property records recorded on or after March 1, 2012, can be searched by block and lot, and/or property owner’s name.

Property records from 1965 to February 2012 can only be searched by the property owner’s name.

Property records before 1965 are available at http://ccarchive.co.burlington.nj.us/NewViewer.aspx and can be searched only by the property owner’s name. “Guest Login” is available without registering.

Maps recorded prior to 1995 are not available on PRESS.

If you have any questions or comments, please contact the office of the Burlington County Clerk at countyclerk@co.burlington.nj.us or 609-265-5122.
